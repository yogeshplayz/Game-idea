##NPCs (Non-Player Characters)

###Quest Givers & Mentors

- **Master Koji** - Age 60, Head of the Magic Academy's Combat Division
  - Role: Provides combat training quests and tactical guidance for dungeon encounters
  - Personality: Stern but fair, respects effort over innate talent—sees Todoshi as a kindred spirit
  - Story Hook: Can unlock advanced swordsmanship techniques if players complete his training challenges
  - Potential Quest: "Training Grounds" - Defeat progressively harder combat scenarios to earn skill upgrades

- **Elder Senna** - Age 75+, Ancient scholar and librarian of the Academy Archives
  - Role: Provides lore quests and rewards knowledge-based items that reveal world secrets
  - Personality: Cryptic and patient, speaks in riddles, but genuinely wants to help those seeking wisdom
  - Story Hook: Holds secrets about Todoshi's condition and alternative power sources
  - Potential Quest: "Lost Tomes" - Retrieve rare magical books scattered throughout dungeons

- **Captain Drake** - Age 45, Commander of the Kingdom's Guard
  - Role: Issues bounty quests to eliminate dungeon threats and rogue mages
  - Personality: Gruff, military-like, but appreciates competence and courage
  - Story Hook: Offers rewards for defeating increasingly dangerous enemies and clearing contaminated areas
  - Potential Quest: "Purge the Blight" - Clear specific dungeons of corrupted creatures before they spread

- **Nurse Hana** - Age 35, Healer and owner of the Wellness Clinic
  - Role: Sells healing items, provides recovery quests, and offers lore about the plague/blight
  - Personality: Warm, motherly, genuinely cares about the health of the community
  - Story Hook: Missions to collect rare medicinal herbs or ingredients for cures
  - Potential Quest: "Epidemic" - Gather disease samples or cure ingredients to help sick NPCs

###Merchants & Crafters

- **Ironsmith Borin** - Age 55, Master blacksmith and weapons crafter
  - Role: Sells and upgrades weapons; can craft items from materials found in dungeons
  - Personality: Gruff but passionate about his craft, appreciates quality materials
  - Story Hook: Will teach weapon crafting for rare ores found in deep dungeons
  - Special Service: Upgrade weapons with found runes or elemental infusions

- **Clothier Aster** - Age 40, Elegant tailor and armor specialist
  - Role: Sells armor, cloaks, and enchanted garments with special properties
  - Personality: Fashionable and charming, loves gossip and information trading
  - Story Hook: Can craft legendary armor sets if you bring rare materials
  - Special Service: Customize armor appearance while maintaining stats

- **Alchemist Sage** - Age 70, Eccentric potion master living in a cluttered shop
  - Role: Sells potions, poisons, and status cures; crafts special elixirs from dungeon ingredients
  - Personality: Absent-minded but brilliant, speaks to his ingredients like they're people
  - Story Hook: Unlock special recipes by bringing rare dungeon plants and minerals
  - Potential Quest: "Ingredient Hunt" - Gather specific plants only found in dangerous areas

- **Jeweler Lyssa** - Age 30, Crafts and sells magical accessories and enchanted items
  - Role: Creates rings, amulets, and trinkets that grant special abilities or stat boosts
  - Personality: Sharp-eyed and detail-oriented, appreciates fine craftsmanship
  - Story Hook: Can create unique items tied to character backstories
  - Special Service: Engrave items with names or special inscriptions for personalization

###Tavern & Social Hub

- **Barkeep Torvin** - Age 50, Owner of the local tavern "The Wandering Griffin"
  - Role: Provides information quests, mission board updates, and social gossip
  - Personality: Knows everyone's business, fatherly to regulars, fair-dealing
  - Story Hook: Overhears crucial plot information; quests often start here
  - Special Feature: Access to hidden mission board with rare contracts

- **Bard Silas** - Age 28, Entertainer and information broker at the tavern
  - Role: Shares stories and legends that provide hints to hidden dungeons and lore
  - Personality: Charming, romantic, and mysterious—knows more than he lets on
  - Story Hook: Songs hint at secret areas; befriending him unlocks lore entries
  - Potential Quest: "Lost Melodies" - Recover stolen musical instruments from dungeons

- **Dice Master Cas** - Age 40, Gambling entrepreneur operating in the tavern
  - Role: Offers optional gambling mini-games and risk/reward betting quests
  - Personality: Smooth-talking, always grinning, plays people like a fiddle
  - Story Hook: Can win rare items through gambling; some quests require high stakes
  - Warning: Can lose money or items if you lose bets

###Dungeon & Combat Related

- **Cartographer Eldin** - Age 55, Explorer and map maker
  - Role: Sells maps revealing hidden areas, secret passages, and treasure locations
  - Personality: Adventure-hungry, curious, loves discovering new places
  - Story Hook: Provides hints about undiscovered dungeons and their dangers
  - Potential Quest: "Uncharted Territories" - Explore new areas and return with findings

- **Beast Tamer Kess** - Age 38, Captures and studies dungeon creatures
  - Role: Provides information about enemy types, weaknesses, and patterns
  - Personality: Eccentric and obsessive about creature behavior, protective of them
  - Story Hook: Unlock bestiary entries; learn enemy weaknesses to make combat easier
  - Special Feature: Can temporarily summon creatures to aid you (for a price)

- **Curse Breaker Morn** - Age 45, Specialist in magical afflictions and contamination
  - Role: Removes status ailments, curses, and corrupted equipment debuffs
  - Personality: Somber and serious, carries the weight of countless curses lifted
  - Story Hook: Offers quests to cleanse corrupted areas and break dark enchantments
  - Potential Quest: "Purification Rite" - Gather materials to break powerful curses

###Lore & Story NPCs

- **Historian Vedric** - Age 80, Scholar studying the origin of mana deficiency syndrome
  - Role: Provides exposition about Todoshi's condition through discoverable documents
  - Personality: Gentle and reflective, passionate about historical accuracy
  - Story Hook: Holds clues to whether mana deficiency is a curse, mutation, or blessing
  - Potential Quest: "Forgotten Archives" - Find historical records about past mana-deficient individuals

- **Wanderer Orin** - Age Unknown, Mysterious traveler with shadowy past
  - Role: Appears randomly in different locations; offers cryptic quests with high rewards
  - Personality: Enigmatic, speaks in half-truths, appears to know more than he reveals
  - Story Hook: Might be connected to Malkov, Todoshi's past, or deeper conspiracies
  - Special Feature: Quests with moral ambiguity—choices matter and affect story branches

- **Street Urchin Pip** - Age 10, Orphan living in the city slums
  - Role: Provides information about street-level happenings; small fetch quests
  - Personality: Quick-witted, resourceful, innocent yet hardened by street life
  - Story Hook: Can become an ally; helping Pip unlocks a hidden character arc
  - Potential Quest: "Protect the Innocent" - Defend Pip from thugs and help find shelter

###Antagonistic NPCs

- **Mage Inquisitor Vorde** - Age 55, Fanatic enforcer of "pure" mana usage
  - Role: Optional boss encounters; offers morally grey quests to eliminate "heretics"
  - Personality: Zealous, bigoted, views anyone like Todoshi as an abomination
  - Story Hook: Serves as a smaller antagonist before confronting Malkov
  - Potential Conflict: Choosing to fight him or reason with him affects reputation

- **Corrupt Noble Stratton** - Age 60, Wealthy merchant funding dark experiments
  - Role: Hidden quest giver with dangerous missions; ties to Malkov's operations
  - Personality: Suave but ruthless, hides cruelty beneath gentlemanly facade
  - Story Hook: Gradually discovered to be working against the heroes' interests
  - Twist Potential: Could be a red herring masking deeper conspiracies
