## Attributes & Mechanics

### Weapon Attributes

Each weapon should have these core stats:

| Attribute | Description | Impact |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Damage** | Base damage output per hit | Determines raw combat power |
| **Attack Speed** | Hits per second (0.5-2.0) | Affects DPS and stamina drain |
| **Range** | Distance weapon reaches (in units) | Determines positioning strategy |
| **Critical Hit Chance** | % chance for increased damage | Varies by weapon type |
| **Critical Multiplier** | Damage multiplier when critical occurs | Set per weapon/type |
| **Knockback Force** | Distance enemy is pushed | More on heavy weapons |
| **Elemental Damage** | Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison, etc. | Adds special effects |
| **Durability** | Uses before breaking (100-500) | Repair costs gold/materials |
| **Weight** | Affects stamina drain | Impacts movement speed |
| **Requirements** | Minimum Strength/Dexterity needed | Prevents low-level use |
| **Rarity** | Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary | Affects drop rate & stats |
| **Affixes** | Prefixes/suffixes that alter behavior | e.g., +Fire, Life steal, Faster reload |
| **Upgrades** | Upgrade slots or upgrade level (0-10) | Increases base stats |

Additional mechanics:
- Weapon scaling: Weapons scale with primary stats (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence) — define soft caps and diminishing returns.
- Weapon techniques: Each weapon gains specialized skills (e.g., spear sweep, katana Iaijutsu) unlocked by use or training.
- Charge attacks: Heavy weapons have charged hits that alter damage/knockback.
- Weapon modes: Some weapons can switch mode (e.g., crossbow to repeating mode, sword to greatsword stance).
- Ranged ammo types: Different ammo for bows/guns (explosive, armor-piercing, poison-tipped, silent darts).
- Dual-wield penalties/bonuses: Dual-wielding increases attack speed but reduces accuracy/critical multiplier unless specialized.

### Armor Attributes

Each armor piece should have:

| Attribute | Description | Impact |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Defense Rating** | Reduces damage by X% or flat amount | Primary survivability stat |
| **Weight** | Affects movement speed penalty | Heavy = -15%, Light = +10% |
| **Elemental Resistance** | % reduction to fire/ice/lightning/poison | Situational protection |
| **Special Effects** | Unique bonuses (reflect damage, heal on hit, etc.) | Set unique builds |
| **Durability** | Uses before repair needed (50-300) | Maintenance cost |
| **Enchantment Slots** | Number of gems that can be added | Customization depth |
| **Rarity** | Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Mythic | Affects stats |
| **Set Bonuses** | Bonus when wearing multiple pieces | Encourages complete sets |
| **Encumbrance** | Combined weight affects roll speed and stamina regen | Systemic mobility impact |

New mechanics:
- Adaptive resistances: Some armor slowly adapts to repeated damage types (e.g., +1% resistance after X hits of same element).
- Active armor skills: Heavy sets may grant a defensive stance or shield bash ability.
- Armor degradation: Heavier armors degrade slower but cost more to repair.
- Modular armor parts: Swapable plates or enchantment sockets that can be rearranged to change stats on the fly.

## Class Builds

### Warrior Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Greatsword or Axe
- **Secondary Weapon**: None
- **Armor**: Full Plate Armor
- **Stats**: Strength (High), Constitution (High), Dexterity (Low)
- **Play Style**: Tank & damage dealer
- **Weakness**: Slow, vulnerable to ranged attacks
- **Strength**: High defense, burst damage

### Rogue Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Dual Daggers or Dagger + Crossbow
- **Secondary Weapon**: Throwing Knives
- **Armor**: Leather Armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (Very High), Agility (High), Strength (Low)
- **Play Style**: Hit-and-run, evasion-focused
- **Weakness**: Low defense, need precise timing
- **Strength**: High mobility, critical strikes

### Mage Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Staff or Wand
- **Secondary Weapon**: Tome (passive)
- **Armor**: Enchanted Robes or Cloth Robes
- **Stats**: Intelligence (Very High), Wisdom (High), Constitution (Low)
- **Play Style**: Crowd control, burst damage from distance
- **Weakness**: Low health, long cast times
- **Strength**: AOE damage, crowd control

### Paladin Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Sword + Mace (or Shield)
- **Secondary Weapon**: None
- **Armor**: Knight's Armor
- **Stats**: Strength (High), Intelligence (High), Constitution (High)
- **Play Style**: Support tank, balanced offense/defense
- **Weakness**: Requires high stat allocation
- **Strength**: Versatile, tankiness + damage

### Ranger Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Bow or Crossbow
- **Secondary Weapon**: Dagger for melee
- **Armor**: Studded Leather or Scaled Armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (High), Wisdom (High), Strength (Moderate)
- **Play Style**: Ranged DPS with melee backup
- **Weakness**: Fragile at close range
- **Strength**: High ranged damage, mobility

### Monk Build (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: None (Unarmed)
- **Secondary Weapon**: Chakram (throwing)
- **Armor**: Light or no armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (High), Wisdom (Very High), Strength (Moderate)
- **Play Style**: Speed & combo-focused, meditation abilities
- **Weakness**: Requires skill to master
- **Strength**: Fastest movement, unblockable combos

### Berserker (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: Two-handed Axe or Warhammer
- **Secondary Weapon**: None
- **Armor**: Medium to Heavy (minimal shields)
- **Stats**: Strength (Very High), Constitution (High), Rage (resource)
- **Play Style**: High-risk high-reward, enters berserk state for bonus damage but reduced defense
- **Weakness**: Less control, vulnerable to kiting
- **Strength**: Massive burst damage and armor penetration

### Necromancer (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: Grimoire or Bone Staff
- **Secondary Weapon**: Summoning Tome
- **Armor**: Enchanted Robes / Spectral Plate
- **Stats**: Intelligence (High), Wisdom (High), Constitution (Low)
- **Play Style**: Pet-summon control, drains and curses
- **Weakness**: Squishy master—pets take damage first
- **Strength**: Control of battlefield through minions and debuffs

### Artificer / Engineer (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: Hand Crossbow / Gadget Staff
- **Secondary Weapon**: Trap Kit
- **Armor**: Brigandine / Runic Vestments
- **Stats**: Intelligence (High), Dexterity (Moderate), Engineering (resource)
- **Play Style**: Builds turrets/traps, applies weapon/armor mods
- **Weakness**: Dependent on gadgets, lower direct damage
- **Strength**: Utility, control, customization

### Beastmaster (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: Spear or Hunter's Bow
- **Secondary Weapon**: Pet Focus (collar)
- **Armor**: Scaled Leather / Studded
- **Stats**: Wisdom (High), Dexterity (High), Strength (Moderate)
- **Play Style**: Pet synergy and taming, hybrid combat
- **Weakness**: Pets can be targeted to disable the build
- **Strength**: Strong summons and battlefield control

### Crafting System Suggestions

- **Base Materials**: Iron, Steel, Mithril, Dragonscale, Obsidian, Adamantine
- **Enchantments**: Add elemental effects, stat bonuses, special abilities
- **Durability**: Items lose effectiveness over time, require gold to repair
- **Upgrades**: Weapons can be upgraded to next tier with rare materials
- **Socketing**: Weapons and armor can have sockets for gems (offense/defense)
- **Transmutation**: Combine two items to create a new item with partial stats from both
- **Artifact Forging**: Endgame system using multiple rare components and quests to create unique legendary gear

### New Crafting Concepts
- **Imbuing**: Temporarily add an elemental effect for a short duration using consumables
- **Affix Re-rolls**: Use special reagents to re-roll a weapon/armour's affix (cost scales with rarity)
- **Masterwork**: Improve weapon/armor baseline and reduce durability loss for a cost and materials
- **Tempering**: Increase damage or defense by investing a percentage of base resource; soft caps apply

### Gem Types (examples)
- **Ruby**: +Fire damage / crit chance
- **Sapphire**: +Mana / spell amplification
- **Emerald**: +Poison / life leech
- **Topaz**: +Lightning / attack speed
- **Onyx**: +Armor penetration / bleed resistance

### Synergy System
Create combinations that reward players:
- **Elemental Sets**: Fire staff + Fire robe = 30% fire damage increase
- **Armor Matching**: Full plate set worn = +10% defense on all pieces
- **Thematic Builds**: All dragon-themed items = dragon abilities unlocked
- **Weapon-Armor Bonds**: Specific legendary weapons gain extra effects when wielded with matching armor set

## Balance Considerations

- **Trade-offs**: Heavy armor = more defense but less mobility
- **Skill vs. Gear**: Skilled rogue should beat unskilled warrior
- **Counters**: Rock-paper-scissors style (Strength beats Armor, Speed beats Strength, Magic beats Speed)
- **Progression**: Early game accessibility, late-game complexity
- **PvP Balance**: Ensure no single build dominates multiplayer

Additional balance notes:
- **Scaling & Soft Caps**: Use diminishing returns to prevent runaway stats (e.g., +stat effectiveness drops after thresholds).
- **Affix Limits**: Limit number of high-power affixes per item to avoid overstacking.
- **Cooldowns**: Powerful abilities should have meaningful cooldowns or resource costs.
- **Enemy Design**: Introduce enemies that punish overreliance on one tactic (e.g., ranged-sniping, anti-pet creatures).
