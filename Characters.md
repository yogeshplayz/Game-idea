#Game characters

##Main Character (MC)
- **Todoshi** - Age 18 years, born with mana deficiency syndrome
  - Story: Todoshi was born with a rare condition that prevents him from accumulating mana, a source of power in his world. Despite his family's high magical status, he has spent his entire life feeling inadequate and worthless. His journey begins when his Grandfather reveals a hidden truth: unconventional power sources exist beyond traditional mana. This discovery becomes his catalyst for proving that he can be a hero despite his limitations. Throughout the story, Todoshi learns that true strength comes from determination, friendship, and creativity rather than innate magical ability.
  - Character Arc: From self-doubt and desperation → embracing his unique path → becoming the symbol of hope for those deemed powerless

##Family
- **Kaname** (Father) - Powerful mage who works at the Magic Academy, struggles with disappointment over Todoshi's condition
  - Story: Kaname initially viewed Todoshi's condition as a family shame, unable to reconcile his son's powerlessness with his own status as a prestigious mage. Early in the story, he appears cold and distant. However, as Todoshi's journey progresses and proves successful, Kaname undergoes a transformation, learning to accept and eventually be proud of his son's unconventional heroism. His redemption arc mirrors Todoshi's growth.

- **Yuki** (Mother) - Healer, supportive and caring, helps Todoshi manage his syndrome
  - Story: Yuki is Todoshi's pillar of emotional support from the beginning. She never doubts her son and works behind the scenes to help him understand and manage his condition. Her love is unconditional and her wisdom often guides Todoshi through his darkest moments. She represents the warmth and acceptance Todoshi needs to believe in himself.

- **Akira** (Younger Sister) - Age 14, naturally talented in magic, looks up to her brother despite his limitations
  - Story: Akira has everything Todoshi doesn't: natural magical talent and the admiration of her peers. Despite having every advantage, she idolizes her older brother for his courage and perseverance. Her admiration roots from Todoshi's refusal to give up, and she often turns to him for guidance despite being younger. Through her eyes, readers witness how Todoshi's struggle inspires those around him.

- **Grandfather** - Retired legendary warrior, becomes Todoshi's mentor and source of hope
  - Story: The Grandfather is a legendary figure from a bygone era who retired from active duty. When he discovers Todoshi's condition, he sees potential where others see weakness. He reveals that he too faced limitations in his prime and learned to overcome them through unconventional methods. He becomes Todoshi's sensei, teaching him martial arts, ancient magic techniques, and the philosophy that true power lies in understanding oneself. His mentorship is the turning point in Todoshi's journey.

##Friends
- **Raze** - Childhood friend, enthusiastic swordsman with unwavering loyalty to Todoshi
  - Story: Raze has been Todoshi's best friend since childhood and has never once treated him differently because of his condition. Raze's unwavering belief in Todoshi is inspiring, often pushing him forward when doubt creeps in. Through their friendship, Raze learns that courage isn't about personal power but about standing by those you care about. His sword skills complement Todoshi's unconventional abilities, making them a formidable team.

- **Mira** - Intelligent mage and scholar, helps Todoshi understand his condition and find alternative power sources
  - Story: Mira is a brilliant scholar who becomes fascinated by Todoshi's case. Rather than viewing his condition as a curse, she approaches it scientifically and discovers that there are alternative energy sources that work for him. Her intellectual support becomes crucial in helping Todoshi understand the "why" behind his powers. Mira represents the bridge between traditional magic and the new possibilities Todoshi embodies.

- **Kota** - Cheerful comic relief character, master of light magic and street smarts
  - Story: Kota comes from the streets and uses humor and charm to survive. He joins the group after being saved by Todoshi and becomes the emotional glue that keeps the team's spirits high during difficult times. His mastery of light magic proves unexpectedly useful in key moments, and his street wisdom often provides practical solutions to problems the group faces.

- **Luna** - Mysterious girl with hidden powers, becomes a close companion and romantic interest
  - Story: Luna appears mysterious and isolated, hiding powerful abilities she doesn't fully understand or control. When she meets Todoshi, she finds someone who doesn't judge her for being different. Their connection deepens as she discovers that her hidden powers are also "unconventional" in their own way. Luna's arc involves learning to accept and control her abilities while finding belonging with Todoshi's group. The romantic tension between them adds an emotional layer to the story.

##Villain
- **Malkov** - Tyrannical dark mage seeking to absorb all mana in the world to become immortal
  - Backstory: Former protégé of Todoshi's grandfather, Malkov was once a promising student who felt rejected when his mentor chose a different path (leading to retirement). Consumed by bitterness and the belief that power is the only truth, Malkov turned to forbidden magic. He became obsessed with immortality and the belief that only by controlling all mana could he achieve true power and respect. His darkness stems from the rejection he experienced and his misguided pursuit of absolute control.
  - Goal: Destroy the Magic Academy, eliminate all free-willed mages, and enslave the remaining magic users to harvest their mana for his immortality ritual
  - Story Arc: Malkov serves as both an external threat and a dark mirror to Todoshi. While Todoshi learns that strength comes from within and unconventional paths, Malkov represents the corruption that comes from seeking power through domination. The climax involves not just defeating Malkov militarily, but helping him—or destroying him—based on whether redemption is possible for someone so consumed by darkness.
  - Weakness: Vulnerable to unconventional magic sources that don't rely on traditional mana. This is the key to Todoshi's victory—Malkov's obsession with traditional mana blinds him to the very power Todoshi has been learning to harness.

##Anti-Hero
- **Vex** - Cunning rogue and black market dealer, initially opposes Todoshi but gradually becomes an unlikely ally
  - Motivation: Profit and survival are Vex's primary drivers. He deals in illegal magical artifacts and information, seeing the world as a transaction. His introduction to the story involves him clashing with Todoshi's group, but gradually he becomes entangled in their mission.
  - Story Arc: Through repeated interactions with Todoshi and his friends, Vex is exposed to genuine friendship and selfless heroism for the first time. He begins to question his philosophy that everyone is self-serving. A turning point comes when he's forced to choose between profit and loyalty, and he chooses loyalty. This act of sacrifice against his nature becomes the foundation of his redemption.
  - Special Skills: Stealth, hacking magical seals, and information gathering make him invaluable to the group
  - Character Development: Vex transitions from self-serving rogue → conflicted ally → someone learning what it means to have principles and be part of something larger than himself. His journey raises the question: "Can redemption exist for those who choose it willingly?"
  - Relationship with Team: Vex remains cynical and sarcastic even as he becomes a hero, never fully losing his rogue edge. His dry humor complements Kota's cheerfulness, and his survival skills often save the day when magic alone isn't enough.
