# Weapons and Armor Ideas

## Weapons

### Melee Weapons
- **Sword**: Classic blade weapon, moderate damage and speed
- **Axe**: Heavy weapon with high damage but slower swing speed
- **Spear**: Long-reach weapon, good for keeping distance from enemies
- **Mace**: Blunt force weapon, effective against armored enemies
- **Dagger**: Fast, low-damage weapon, good for quick attacks and combos
- **Greatsword**: Two-handed sword with high damage and knockback

### Ranged Weapons
- **Bow**: Standard ranged weapon, moderate damage and fire rate
- **Crossbow**: Slower fire rate but higher damage per shot
- **Throwing Knives**: Fast projectiles with low damage
- **Blowgun**: Silent ranged option, good for stealth gameplay

### Magic Weapons
- **Staff**: Focuses magical energy, allows casting spells
- **Wand**: Faster casting speed than staff, lower damage
- **Tome**: Enhances spell damage and unlock special abilities

### Special Weapons
- **Whip**: Can hit multiple enemies, disarm opponents
- **Chain**: Mid-range weapon with unique swing patterns
- **Javelin**: Heavy throwing weapon, causes knockback

---

## Armor

### Light Armor
- **Leather Armor**: Low defense, high mobility, minimal movement penalty
- **Cloth Robes**: Minimal defense, best for magic users, no movement penalty
- **Studded Leather**: Reinforced leather with better protection

### Medium Armor
- **Chain Mail**: Balanced defense and mobility
- **Plate Armor (Partial)**: Chest and leg plates, moderate defense
- **Scaled Armor**: Dragon scales, good protection with decent mobility

### Heavy Armor
- **Full Plate Armor**: Maximum defense, significant movement penalty
- **Knight's Armor**: Ornate plate with enchantment slots
- **Obsidian Plate**: Rare heavy armor with high defense and special properties

### Magical Armor
- **Mithril Armor**: Lightweight but strong, resistance to certain damage types
- **Enchanted Robes**: Provides magical resistance and spell amplification
- **Spectral Plate**: Ethereal armor that phases through some attacks

---

## Armor Attributes

Each armor piece should have:
- **Defense Rating**: Reduces incoming damage by a percentage or flat amount
- **Weight**: Affects movement speed and stamina drain
- **Special Effects**: Unique bonuses (fire resistance, magic amplification, etc.)
- **Durability**: How much use before it needs repair
- **Rarity**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary

---

## Weapon Attributes

Each weapon should have:
- **Damage**: Base damage output
- **Attack Speed**: How fast the weapon can be used
- **Range**: How far the weapon reaches
- **Special Effects**: Critical hit chance, knockback, elemental damage, etc.
- **Durability**: How much use before it needs repair
- **Requirements**: Minimum stats needed to use effectively
- **Rarity**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary

---

## Armor and Weapon Combinations

### Warrior Build
- Weapon: Greatsword or Axe
- Armor: Full Plate Armor

### Rogue Build
- Weapon: Dagger + Crossbow or Dual Daggers
- Armor: Leather Armor

### Mage Build
- Weapon: Staff or Wand
- Armor: Enchanted Robes or Cloth Robes

### Paladin Build
- Weapon: Sword + Mace
- Armor: Knight's Armor

### Ranger Build
- Weapon: Bow or Crossbow
- Armor: Studded Leather or Scaled Armor
