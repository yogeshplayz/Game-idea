## Attributes & Mechanics

### Weapon Attributes

Each weapon should have these core stats:

| Attribute | Description | Impact |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Damage** | Base damage output per hit | Determines raw combat power |
| **Attack Speed** | Hits per second (0.5-2.0) | Affects DPS and stamina drain |
| **Range** | Distance weapon reaches (in units) | Determines positioning strategy |
| **Critical Hit Chance** | % chance for increased damage | Varies by weapon type |
| **Knockback Force** | Distance enemy is pushed | More on heavy weapons |
| **Elemental Damage** | Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison, etc. | Adds special effects |
| **Durability** | Uses before breaking (100-500) | Repair costs gold/materials |
| **Weight** | Affects stamina drain | Impacts movement speed |
| **Requirements** | Minimum Strength/Dexterity needed | Prevents low-level use |
| **Rarity** | Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary | Affects drop rate & stats |

### Armor Attributes

Each armor piece should have:

| Attribute | Description | Impact |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Defense Rating** | Reduces damage by X% or flat amount | Primary survivability stat |
| **Weight** | Affects movement speed penalty | Heavy = -15%, Light = +10% |
| **Elemental Resistance** | % reduction to fire/ice/lightning/poison | Situational protection |
| **Special Effects** | Unique bonuses (reflect damage, heal on hit, etc.) | Set unique builds |
| **Durability** | Uses before repair needed (50-300) | Maintenance cost |
| **Enchantment Slots** | Number of gems that can be added | Customization depth |
| **Rarity** | Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Mythic | Affects stats |
| **Set Bonuses** | Bonus when wearing multiple pieces | Encourages complete sets |

## Class Builds

### Warrior Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Greatsword or Axe
- **Secondary Weapon**: None
- **Armor**: Full Plate Armor
- **Stats**: Strength (High), Constitution (High), Dexterity (Low)
- **Play Style**: Tank & damage dealer
- **Weakness**: Slow, vulnerable to ranged attacks
- **Strength**: High defense, burst damage

### Rogue Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Dual Daggers or Dagger + Crossbow
- **Secondary Weapon**: Throwing Knives
- **Armor**: Leather Armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (Very High), Agility (High), Strength (Low)
- **Play Style**: Hit-and-run, evasion-focused
- **Weakness**: Low defense, need precise timing
- **Strength**: High mobility, critical strikes

### Mage Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Staff or Wand
- **Secondary Weapon**: Tome (passive)
- **Armor**: Enchanted Robes or Cloth Robes
- **Stats**: Intelligence (Very High), Wisdom (High), Constitution (Low)
- **Play Style**: Crowd control, burst damage from distance
- **Weakness**: Low health, long cast times
- **Strength**: AOE damage, crowd control

### Paladin Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Sword + Mace (or Shield)
- **Secondary Weapon**: None
- **Armor**: Knight's Armor
- **Stats**: Strength (High), Intelligence (High), Constitution (High)
- **Play Style**: Support tank, balanced offense/defense
- **Weakness**: Requires high stat allocation
- **Strength**: Versatile, tankiness + damage

### Ranger Build
- **Primary Weapon**: Bow or Crossbow
- **Secondary Weapon**: Dagger for melee
- **Armor**: Studded Leather or Scaled Armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (High), Wisdom (High), Strength (Moderate)
- **Play Style**: Ranged DPS with melee backup
- **Weakness**: Fragile at close range
- **Strength**: High ranged damage, mobility

### Monk Build (New)
- **Primary Weapon**: None (Unarmed)
- **Secondary Weapon**: Chakram (throwing)
- **Armor**: Light or no armor
- **Stats**: Dexterity (High), Wisdom (Very High), Strength (Moderate)
- **Play Style**: Speed & combo-focused, meditation abilities
- **Weakness**: Requires skill to master
- **Strength**: Fastest movement, unblockable combos

## Progression & Crafting

### Weapon Tiers by Level

| Tier | Levels | Rarity | Example | Base Damage |
|------|--------|--------|---------|------------|
| Beginner | 1-10 | Common | Iron Sword | 5-10 |
| Novice | 11-20 | Uncommon | Steel Axe | 15-20 |
| Apprentice | 21-35 | Rare | Enchanted Staff | 25-35 |
| Expert | 36-50 | Legendary | Dragonbane Sword | 40-60 |
| Master | 51+ | Mythic | Godslayer's Greatsword | 75-100 |

### Crafting System Suggestions

- **Base Materials**: Iron, Steel, Mithril, Dragonscale
- **Enchantments**: Add elemental effects, stat bonuses, special abilities
- **Durability**: Items lose effectiveness over time, require gold to repair
- **Upgrades**: Weapons can be upgraded to next tier with rare materials

### Synergy System

Create combinations that reward players:
- **Elemental Sets**: Fire staff + Fire robe = 30% fire damage increase
- **Armor Matching**: Full plate set worn = +10% defense on all pieces
- **Thematic Builds**: All dragon-themed items = dragon abilities unlocked

## Balance Considerations

- **Trade-offs**: Heavy armor = more defense but less mobility
- **Skill vs. Gear**: Skilled rogue should beat unskilled warrior
- **Counters**: Rock-paper-scissors style (Strength beats Armor, Speed beats Strength, Magic beats Speed)
- **Progression**: Early game accessibility, late-game complexity
- **PvP Balance**: Ensure no single build dominates multiplayer
