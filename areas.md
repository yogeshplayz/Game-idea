##Area

**Fire (North) — The Ashveil Dominion** is a volcanic, war-hardened land where fire is a language and culture, not just a weapon. The Emberi are forge-masters who carve cities into caldera walls.

**Water (South) — The Tidesong Expanse** is an archipelago civilization built on oral memory and tidal prophecy. The Marathi encode navigation into song and fight with fluid-form combat.

**Earth (Central) — The Rootstone Heartlands** are the world's oldest lands, home to the Terrath — patient scholars who build as deep as they build tall, dwelling inside a world-sized geode.

**Wind (East) — The Galereach Wilds** are a nomadic frontier of sculpted mesas and dune seas, where stillness is an omen and the Zephrin travel vast distances by kite-glider.

**Lightning (West) — The Stormvault Coast** is the most electrically dangerous place alive, and the Volthari have harnessed it — their copper-spired cities run on captured storm energy.

**Light (Floating Island) — The Luminarch Isles** drift above the clouds, home to the truth-keeping Aurelith whose light magic makes deception impossible within their borders.

**Dark (Underworld) — The Hollowed Below** is a vast subterranean realm where the Umbrath treat darkness as potential rather than void, and Shadowweavers build structures from solidified shadow.
