# Epilogue

This is a world where humans are able to control elements.

# Act 1: The Awakening and Discovery

## Scene 1: Emergence from Darkness

As consciousness slowly returns, the protagonist staggers to his feet, brushing away layers of golden sand. The devastated landscape stretches endlessly before him—towering dunes that seem to touch the sky and vast canyons carved by winds that no longer follow natural laws.

A figure approaches, moving with deliberate purpose through the wasteland. This is Kael, a legendary hero whispered about in ancient texts—one of the few who learned to master the elements before the Cataclysm nearly destroyed civilization itself.

"You've finally awakened," Kael's voice cuts through the wind like a blade. He extends his hand, a warm aura of flames dancing around his palm. "The world was not always like this. Once, we had balance. Once, we had cities and kingdoms. Then everything changed."

The protagonist stares at the flames without fear, mesmerized by their beauty. He reaches out instinctively, and the fire doesn't burn him. Instead, it swirls around his fingers like a living thing, responding to his will.

## Scene 2: First Steps in a Changed World

With Kael as his guide, the protagonist begins his journey across the transformed landscape. Their first stop is the remains of what was once a thriving city. Now, nothing but crumbling stone structures and wind-carved monuments remain.

As they trek through the ruins, the protagonist begins to notice something peculiar—pockets of life persisting in impossible places. A grove of crystalline plants grows where water once flowed. Creatures adapted to the new world scurry between the stones, their forms twisted but purposeful.

"The elements are awakening in those around us," Kael explains, gesturing toward a figure in the distance. "Not all survived the Cataclysm. Those who did... they changed. Some learned to harness the raw magic in the air. Others were consumed by it."

The protagonist watches as a young girl with hands wreathed in water vapor tends to the crystalline plants, coaxing them to grow. She looks up, sees them, and fear flickers in her eyes before she turns and flees.

## Scene 3: Encounters with the New World

Days pass as the protagonist and Kael traverse the ruined world. Each encounter teaches him something new:

**The Sand Tribes**: A nomadic people who have mastered earth manipulation, creating mobile settlements that shift and flow like sand dunes. They are suspicious of outsiders but show Kael respect, remembering his role in the old wars.

**The Drowning Gardens**: Once a lush paradise, this region is now a chaotic mix of floating islands suspended above an endless chasm where water falls upward in defiance of nature. A woman named Lyra, who commands water with grace, shows them the wonders and horrors of this transformed land.

**The Obsidian Peaks**: Dark volcanic mountains that pierce the sky like a giant's teeth. Here, they encounter Fire-dwellers—fierce warriors who have made their home in the lava flows. The protagonist begins to understand that the world has not ended; it has evolved.

## Scene 4: The Awakening of Power

As the protagonist journeys, he begins to experience strange visions—fragments of a life he can't quite grasp. In his dreams, he sees himself commanding elements with authority, standing alongside Kael and others in robes of power.

One evening, while camping near the Obsidian Peaks, the protagonist becomes frustrated with his amnesia. In his anger, the earth beneath him trembles. Sand rises in a spiraling vortex around him. Wind howls at his command. And for a brief moment, the stars themselves seem to dim as his power awakens.

He is an Elemental Master—someone who can command all four elements, not just one.

Kael watches from a distance, tears streaming down his weathered face. "So the legends were true," he whispers. "The one who fell must rise again. But will he remember in time? The old powers stir once more."

## Scene 5: A Choice Revealed

On the precipice of a great chasm, Kael finally tells the protagonist the truth: he is not merely a survivor of the Cataclysm—he was there when it happened. He was one of the four Elemental Kings, masters of magic tasked with maintaining balance across the realm.

"The world needs you," Kael says. "Not the fallen king you were, but the hero you can become. The darkness that caused the Cataclysm did not die with the old order. It slumbers, gathering strength, waiting for the moment to rise again."

The protagonist looks across the transformed world—at the adapted creatures, the surviving civilizations, the raw power that courses through his veins. The world has changed him as much as he has the power to change it.

He extends his hand to Kael, not as a supplicant accepting help, but as someone ready to forge their own path.

"Then let me discover the truth myself. I will walk this world, meet these people, understand what happened. And when I'm ready, I'll decide what comes next."

With those words, the true journey begins.

# Act 2: The Rising Darkness

## Scene 1: Whispers of a Threat

Weeks into their journey, the protagonist and Kael reach the Crystalline Archive—an ancient repository of knowledge preserved by the remnants of the old civilization. Here, scholars and mages work tirelessly to recover lost texts and understand the nature of the Cataclysm.

An elderly mage named Serah pulls them aside in a candlelit chamber. Her hands tremble as she speaks: "There are reports... sightings of dark forces gathering in the Shattered Wastes. Entire settlements have gone silent. And whatever is causing this, it's moving with purpose."

Kael's expression hardens. "Impossible. He was sealed away..."

"Who?" the protagonist asks, though a strange sense of foreboding washes over him.

Kael exchanges a knowing glance with Serah. "Come. You need to understand the full history of what came before the Cataclysm."

## Scene 2: The Tale of Malkov

In the Archive's deepest chamber, surrounded by ancient scrolls and faded tapestries, Kael reveals the truth. Malkov was once like Kael—a prodigy of the elements, chosen to guard the balance of magic across the realm.

"He was brilliant," Kael begins, his voice heavy with regret. "I trained him alongside the other Kings. We were bound by oath to protect the realm and ensure no single force could dominate the elements or the mana that flows through all things."

Serah continues the tale: "When Kael and the Kings refused to support his vision of an eternal reign, Malkov attempted to take the mana by force. The conflict that followed—the Cataclysm—nearly destroyed everything. Millions died. Civilizations fell. And we paid the price of our failure to stop him."

The protagonist feels a chill run down his spine. "You think he's escaping?"

"Not escaping," Kael corrects grimly. "Being freed. And that means there are still followers of his dark vision. There are those who seek to restore him, to bring about the new order he promised."

## Scene 3: Encounter with Darkness

That night, as the protagonist meditates on his newfound connection to the elements, he senses something vast and malevolent probing at the edges of his consciousness—like a great shadow searching for him.

He gasps awake to find that all the plants in the Archive have wilted. The very air feels colder.

Kael stands at the window, watching as distant lightning tears across the sky in unnatural colors—blacks and deep purples that defy nature. "He knows you exist now," Kael says quietly. "An Elemental Master awakening is something he would sense across any distance."

The protagonist stands beside his mentor. "Then we don't wait for him to come to us."

"No," Kael agrees. "But first, we need allies. Malkov has agents throughout the world, people who still believe in his promise of power without limit. We need to unite the scattered kingdoms and peoples before his darkness consumes them all."

## Scene 4: The Gathering Storm

Over the following days, reports accelerate. A caravan is found completely drained of life—all inhabitants mummified, their mana extracted. An entire tribe in the Sand Wastes awakens to find their children stolen in the night, their elemental abilities gone.

The protagonist witnesses it firsthand when they encounter one such creature—a massive wolf wreathed in dark energy that moves with intelligence rather than instinct. During the battle, he hears something that chills him to his core:

"*So the fallen King awakens at last. How delicious. You will either join me in my ascendancy, or you will feed my growing power. Either way, the world will bow before us.*"

Even as Kael defeats the creature, the protagonist realizes the voice doesn't belong to any physical form nearby. Malkov is watching. Malkov is hunting. And his reach is already longer than they feared.

## Scene 5: A New Purpose

In a war council with leaders from the surviving kingdoms—fire-dwellers from the Obsidian Peaks, water-mages from the Drowning Gardens, earth-shapers from the Sand Tribes, and wind-speakers from the high plateaus—the protagonist stands before them, no longer an amnesiac wanderer but a force of destiny.

"Malkov seeks to consume all mana, to remake the world in darkness," the protagonist declares, finding strength in the clarity of purpose. "We cannot stop him alone, but together—united in common cause—we can. We must."

The chamber erupts in voices—some pledging support, others expressing doubt. But a consensus forms. An alliance is forged. And for the first time, the protagonist begins to see what kind of hero he must become.

However, as the council concludes, a messenger arrives with urgent news: "Malkov's forces have already reached the outer settlements. They don't just take mana—they leave behind darkness that corrupts the very land itself."

Kael and the protagonist exchange looks. "Then that's where we'll make our stand," the protagonist says.

The race against darkness has begun.
