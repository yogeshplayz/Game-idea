# Game Progression Route

## Overview
This document outlines the progression path for the main character **Todoshi** throughout the game, from his early struggles to his final confrontation with Malkov.

---

## Act 1: The Beginning (Establishing the Conflict)

### Phase 1.1: Introduction & Self-Discovery
- **Starting Point:** Todoshi's home/family residence
- **Main Objective:** Accept your mana deficiency and begin your journey
- **Key Characters Met:**
  - Raze (Childhood friend) - Convinced to join the quest
  - Akira (Younger sister) - Emotional farewell scene
  - Kaname (Father) - Reconciliation moment
  - Yuki (Mother) - Emotional support before departure
  
### Phase 1.2: Magic Academy
- **Location:** Magic Academy/Library
- **Main Objective:** Uncover the truth about your condition
- **Key Characters Met:**
  - Mira (Scholar friend) - Discovers "Internal Resonance" as alternative power source
  - Master Koji (Combat mentor) - Begins combat training
- **Learning Mechanics:**
  - Unlock basic sword combat techniques
  - Discover the concept of Internal Resonance
  - Develop character-specific mechanics (non-mana based abilities)

---

## Act 2: The Trials (Dungeon Progression)

### Phase 2.1: First Dungeon
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Medium
- **Objective:** Gather resources and prove yourself
- **Companions:** Raze + 1 additional party member
- **Mechanics Introduced:**
  - Basic dungeon exploration
  - Environmental puzzles
  - Simple combat encounters
- **Rewards:** Equipment, experience, resources for training

### Phase 2.2: Mid-Game Dungeons (3-5 dungeons)
- **Difficulty:** Medium to Hard
- **Objective:** Grow stronger, recruit allies, and uncover information about Malkov
- **Companions Recruited:**
  - Mira (Magic support)
  - Kota (Light spell user, comic relief)
  - Luna (Mysterious companion with internal darkness - reaches out at rest points)
  - Vex (Reluctant ally - recruited through morality-based quest)

### Phase 2.3: Character Development Events
- **Rest Stops Between Dungeons:** Character conversations trigger
  - Kota's comic relief moments keep morale high
  - Luna's internal struggle deepens (voices getting louder)
  - Todoshi mentors Luna, building trust
  
### Phase 2.4: Moral Choice Point
- **Quest:** Steal from Corrupt Noble Stratton
- **Choice Consequence:**
  - Recruiting Vex as an ally depends on player persuasion
  - Demonstrates that Todoshi believes people can change
  - Sets up potential character arc for Vex

---

## Act 3: The Preparation (Training & Strengthening)

### Phase 3.1: Advanced Training
- **Location:** Training grounds
- **Mentor:** Grandfather (Legendary mentor)
- **Focus:** 
  - Mastering Internal Resonance abilities
  - Learning that struggle is strength
  - Discovering Todoshi's unique combat advantages
- **Mechanic:** Unlock special techniques unavailable to mana-users

### Phase 3.2: Party Strengthening
- **Objective:** Max out companion relationships
- **Activities:**
  - Training sessions with Master Koji
  - Tavern scenes with Kota (post-dungeon)
  - Late-night conversations with Luna
  - Quest missions with Vex

### Phase 3.3: Knowledge Gathering
- **Objective:** Research Malkov's history and weaknesses
- **Key Information:** 
  - Malkov is centuries old with vast mana reserves
  - His centuries of experience may make him arrogant
  - Grandfather's wisdom: "He'll underestimate you because he's forgotten what struggle feels like"

---

## Act 4: The Final Confrontation (Endgame)

### Phase 4.1: The Final Dungeon
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Location:** Malkov's domain
- **Objectives:**
  - Reach Malkov's inner sanctum
  - Defeat powerful minions/guardians
  - Navigate complex puzzles

### Phase 4.2: The Battle with Malkov
- **Boss Fight:** Malkov (Main antagonist)
- **Combat Mechanics:**
  - Malkov relies heavily on mana-based attacks (powerful but predictable)
  - Todoshi's Internal Resonance abilities prove effective against him
  - Todoshi's lack of mana becomes his greatest strength
  - Party support is crucial (coordinated team attacks)

### Phase 4.3: Resolution
- **Post-Battle:**
  - Reconciliation with Luna (overcoming her internal darkness)
  - Recognition from companions (Raze, Mira, Kota, Vex)
  - Final conversation with family (if alive)
  - Epilogue scene establishing peace

---

## Character Development Milestones

### Todoshi's Journey
1. **Beginning:** Doubts himself, feels powerless
2. **Mid-Game:** Discovers Internal Resonance, grows in confidence
3. **Late-Game:** Becomes a leader, mentors others (especially Luna)
4. **End-Game:** Fully accepts his unique strength and defeats Malkov

### Luna's Arc
1. **Recruitment:** Mysterious companion with internal struggle
2. **Mid-Game:** Voices grow louder, threatens to consume her
3. **Late-Game:** Todoshi's belief in her gives her strength
4. **End-Game:** Overcomes her darkness with party support

### Vex's Arc
1. **Reluctant Ally:** Cynical about changing
2. **Mid-Game:** Gradually believes in the cause
3. **Late-Game:** Fully committed to the team
4. **End-Game:** Proves change is possible

## Optional Content & Side Quests

### Character Relationship Quests
- **Raze:** Childhood memory side quests
- **Mira:** Research expeditions to uncover Internal Resonance lore
- **Master Koji:** Training challenges with increasing difficulty
- **Grandfather:** Wisdom-sharing sessions that unlock special moves

### Moral Choice Side Quests
- Help NPCs using Todoshi's unique abilities
- Recruit additional optional party members
- Unlock special equipment tied to character relationships

## Progression Mechanics

### Experience & Growth
- **Combat Experience:** Earned from defeating enemies
- **Internal Resonance Progression:** Unlocked through story milestones
- **Relationship Levels:** Increase through character interactions and quests
- **Equipment Upgrades:** Found in dungeons or crafted through side quests

### Story Branching
- **Moral Choices:** Affect which characters join your party
- **Relationship Outcomes:** Determine support abilities in final battle
- **Character Arcs:** Multiple endings based on recruitment and relationship progression
